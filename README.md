## 🕹️ About

Phantom Blade Zero is a fast-paced action RPG from S-GAME, blending classic Wuxia storytelling with a dark, machine-infused Kungfupunk world. Players become Soul, an elite assassin with 66 days to uncover a deadly consalternative and confront powerful enemies. Join this beta testing opportunity, help shape the experience, and receive a free game key.

Phantom Blade Zero is for players who enjoy demanding action RPGs, cinematic martial-arts battles, dark fantasy worlds, and story-driven adventures.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Wuxia Action Combat** — Engage enemies with fast, precise, cinematic martial-arts combat.
- 🩸 **Soul’s Final Countdown** — Race against a 66-day limit to uncover the truth behind Soul’s downfall.
- 🏮 **Kungfupunk World** — Explore a striking world where Chinese martial arts meet machines and occult forces.
- 🗡️ **Assassin’s Journey** — Fend off assassins, former comrades, powerful foes, and inhuman monstrosities.
- 🎭 **Dark Consalternative** — Unravel a world-ending plot while defending Soul’s values with his life.
- 🎮 **Unreal Engine 5** — Experience a visually ambitious action adventure powered by Unreal Engine 5.
